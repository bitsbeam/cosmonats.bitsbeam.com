Integrations

Sentry

The middleware wraps every job execution in a Sentry transaction (queue.cosmonats), and the error handler captures every error Cosmo rescues — failed jobs, stream batches, fetches, the scheduler — with its context under cosmonats. No other setup beyond having sentry-ruby initialized.
