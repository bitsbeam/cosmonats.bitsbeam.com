Monitoring

#Web UI

Mount Cosmo::Web (see Installation) for a live, htmx-powered dashboard with a light, dark, or automatic (system) theme. Fonts and assets are bundled, so it loads nothing from a CDN:

  • Overview — processed, failed, busy, enqueued, retries, scheduled and dead totals; a 7- or 30-day throughput chart of successful and failed runs; a grid of every worker thread, busy ones colored by stream; the queues; and the a sample of the jobs running right now
  • Jobs — each job queue with its size, busy jobs and redeliveries, paused and resumed in place; then busy, enqueued (per queue), scheduled and dead jobs, with retry and delete for dead ones and their error and backtrace
  • Processes — every live worker (idle ones included): host, pid, IP, NATS connection name, command line, subscribed streams, busy/concurrency, running/quiet/stopping state, memory, NATS RTT and reconnects. Each worker beats into the _cosmoprocesses KV bucket every 10s, and the jobs it’s running into _cosmojobs; a crashed worker drops out within 60s. Busy jobs come from those heartbeats, so they show up to 10s late, and a job shorter than that may not show at all. The page reads only the processes it shows, and every count is answered by NATS from the keys.
  • Streams — every stream with its subjects, messages, size, retention and replicas, its consumers’ pending, ack-pending and redelivered counts, and pause/resume
  • Crons — every schedule deployed in NATS, with run-now and delete
  • Batches — open and finished batches with their progress, succeeded/failed/pending counts and callbacks
  • Metrics — per job class over today, 7, or 30 days: runs, failures, average execution time (successful runs), and average wait from enqueue to first delivery, plus a per-day chart of runs and average execution time. Recorded by Cosmo::Middleware::Metrics, flushed by every worker’s heartbeat into the internal _cosmometrics counters stream; turn it off with config.metrics.enabled = false

#Job ID links

Every job ID in the Jobs tables can link out, e.g. to the job’s logs in Grafana. Give config.web.jid_link a URL template, whose %{jid}, %{class} and %{stream} are filled in URL-escaped (any other % in it is left as is), or a callable given the job’s payload (:jid, :class, :args, … and :stream) that returns the URL, or nil to keep that one as plain text. Links open in a new tab; only http(s) URLs are linked, and a raising callable falls back to plain text:

#Batch ID links

config.web.bid_link does the same for batch IDs on the Batches page: a URL template with %{bid} and %{parent_id}, or a callable given :bid, :parent_id and :description.

#Refreshing

Each panel refreshes COSMO_WEB_POLL_INTERVAL seconds after its previous response, never on top of one still running, backs off while refreshes fail, and pauses in a hidden tab. Identical refreshes from several viewers share a single NATS read for COSMO_WEB_CACHE_TTL seconds, and while NATS is unreachable the UI answers at once instead of holding your app server’s threads.
