Upgrading

#0.7 → 0.8

0.8 renames Cosmo’s internal NATS storage and changes the dead stream’s retention. Nothing is migrated automatically; these steps carry an existing deployment over. Run the nats commands against your production context.

Before deploying

  • Remove scheduled and dead from setup.jobs and consumers.jobs in config/cosmo.yml, and batch_expiry (now config.batches.expiry in Cosmo.configure). Cosmo creates and tunes those streams itself.
  • Replace require "cosmo/sentry/auto" with:
  • Replace any Cosmo::Config.set(...) with cosmo.yml entries or Cosmo.configure.
  • Deploy while no batch is open and no concurrency-limited job is running: batch state and limit slots move to new buckets, which old and new workers don’t share during a rolling deploy.
